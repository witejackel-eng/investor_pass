## A. Identity
- Name / Aliases: Guy Spier (social: @gspier); "The Education of a Value Investor" author
- Firms / Vehicles: Aquamarine Capital Management LLC / Aquamarine Fund — founded 1997 in Zurich, "closely modeled on the original Buffett Partnerships" (guyspier.com); VALUEx Zurich (founder); co-founded TEDxZürich
- Active period: 1997–2026. **Wind-down announced February 2026** in the final (2025) annual letter: converting to a family office after a cancer diagnosis (grade 4 glioblastoma per Whitney Tilson's summary). Fund returned 11.3% in 2025; cumulative return since inception reported at 1,185.6% (Opalesque).
- Key premise notes (corrections, cautions):
  - **Annual letter archive verified free**: aquamarinefund.com/annual-letter-to-investors lists Annual Letters 2024→2010, each hosted on DocSend (aqfd.docsend.com/view/...) — the "2010–2024 DocSend archive" premise is exactly right. The final 2025 letter is posted at aquamarinefund.com/annual-letter-to-partners-2025.
  - **The "final 2025 DocSend letter" premise verified**: final letter ~15 pages, titled per Opalesque "This Is Not the Letter I Wanted to Be Writing…"; distributed Feb 2026; wind-down after 28 years; family-office conversion.
  - The 2008 Buffett lunch premise verified: Spier + Mohnish Pabrai won the June 2007 Glide charity auction with a record $650,100 bid; lunch took place June 25, 2008 (CNBC dated the lunch coverage then; Spier's own essay is dated Jun 30, 2008).
  - **Podcast credits resolved**: TEDx credit PARTIALLY verified — he "spoke at TED India and co-hosted TEDx Zurich" (MIT Sloan bio; Talking Billings: "co-founded TEDxZürich") — i.e., no famous solo TEDx talk. "Invest Like the Best" and "Masters in Business" credits: **verified-negative this session** — no Spier episode found on either series; do not import. Actual confirmed interview venues: The Investor's Podcast (May 21, 2025 lunch episode; TIP492 was about Nick Sleep, not Spier), Talking Billings Ep. 11 (Jan 30, 2026), MIT Sloan Investment Conference 2026 speaker, CNBC Jun 5, 2026 segment.
  - The "Observer op-ed" item: Observer hosts a Spier author page featuring his "How a $650,100 Lunch With Warren Buffett Changed My Life" essay (2015, book-promo period — also ran in TIME). The bigger op-ed of record is the Bloomberg Opinion piece "The Golden Age of Value Investing Is Over" (Sep 18, 2025; republished by AFR Sep 25, 2025). Record both; do not conflate.
