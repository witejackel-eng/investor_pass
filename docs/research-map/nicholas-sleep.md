# Research Findings — Nicholas Sleep
Research date: 2026 (Task 1-d)

## A. Identity
- Name / Aliases: Nicholas "Nick" Sleep (with partner Qais "Zak" Zakaria; the pair self-deprecatingly ask to be thought of as "deep-burrowing earthworms" in the official letter preamble)
- Firms / Vehicles: Nomad Investment Partnership (launched 2001 under the Marathon Asset Management umbrella; spun out ~2006; liquidated early 2014 with capital returned); I.G.Y. Foundation (UK Charitable Incorporated Organisation — Sleep's charity, now authorized home of the letters)
- Active period: 2001–2014 (letters written every six months from end-2001 to early 2014; **final letter December 2013** — verified verbatim in the authorized PDF postamble: "When we wrote the December 2013 letter, we did not know that it would be our last but, a few months later, the portfolio had been liquidated, funds returned to our partners")
- Key premise notes (corrections, cautions):
  - Premise confirmed: final letter is Dec 2013; the "2001–2013 run" is right, with wind-up executed early 2014. The IGY collection is titled "2001–2014" because the postamble/closure commentary is dated Spring 2021 and the wind-up ran into 2014.
  - Performance: FT reports 921.1% cumulative / 18.4% p.a. after performance fees over the 12 years to 2013; the authors' own preamble (2021) says "all but inevitable that our annual performance will be around twenty percent for twenty years."
  - Standing caution upheld: the "Zambezi"/Africa Sleep investments remain unverified — nothing found this session; treat as unconfirmed.
  - Post-Nomad: Sleep became a school governor in Southeast London and runs the IGY Foundation (philanthropy); he "has not entirely left investing" (postamble).
