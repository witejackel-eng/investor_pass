## A. Identity
- Name / Aliases: François Rochon (Montreal, Québec); the model composite he runs is formally "the Rochon Global Portfolio"
- Firms / Vehicles: Giverny Capital Inc. (Montreal, founded end-1998; named after Monet's Giverny — art-collector founder); Giverny Capital Asset Management LLC (Manhattan, est. 2020); US office in Princeton, NJ since 2009. Partners: Jean-Philippe Bouchard (2002), Nicolas L'Écuyer and Karine Primeau (2005); US directors Patrick Léger and David Poppe (ex-Ruane Cunniff).
- Active period: managing the family/model portfolio since **July 1, 1993** (license formalized 1997; Giverny firm founded 1998); letters continue through the 2025 annual letter (posted March 2026).
- Key premise notes (corrections, cautions):
  - **"Giverny CAP since 1993" nuance resolved**: the since-1993 record belongs to the *Rochon Global Portfolio* — "a real portfolio: a private group of family accounts managed by François Rochon which serves as a model portfolio" (givernycapital.com/en/returns). The Giverny firm itself dates from 1998. CAGR 15.3% vs 9.5% benchmark since 7/1/1993 (as of the 2020 letter) = ~5.9%/yr of outperformance; still ~14.5–15% annualized through 2023–25 per secondary trackers.
  - **Domain**: givernycapital.com is the firm site (EN/FR); francoisrochon.com is his personal Squarespace bio site (French). The live English letters page currently hosts **2017–2025** only — the 2001–2016 letters are NOT on the live page and will need Wayback/third-party recovery, contrary to the "2001–2025 all free" premise (which holds only via mirrors/archives).
  - **RWH016 identified**: episode code for "The Best of the Best w/ François Rochon" — The Investor's Podcast Network's *Richer, Wiser, Happier* series (host William Green), Nov 12, 2022, 1h40m.
  - **Gosselin biography identified**: "François Rochon. Le parcours singulier d'un investisseur d'exception" by André Gosselin (Guy Saint-Jean Éditeur, Feb 1, 2023, ISBN 9782898272707) — French-language biography; purchase item, as the master plan assumed.
