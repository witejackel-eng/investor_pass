# Product Constitution

The binding product document for Investor/Pass. Product and scope decisions
resolve here; when a proposal conflicts with this document, the document wins.

## North Star

> This is where I study investor thinking.

## Retention goal

> This is where I keep my investor research.

Acquisition gets a visitor to open a page. Retention is the product: follows,
saved searches, collections, reading progress, new-since-last-visit, and the
personal home exist so that a researcher who leaves has a reason to return —
and a place to pick up exactly where they stopped.

## Core principle

> Every click must advance: who else → what was said → what was done → what
> changed → what to investigate next.

A page that does not move the researcher along that chain is decoration.
Navigation, cross-links, compare, and the decision ledger are the product;
paragraphs of prose are not.

## Product definition

> Investor/Pass is a source-first research operating system for studying how
> exceptional investors thought, what they documented, what they did, how their
> ideas evolved, where their records overlap or differ, and what the researcher
> wants to follow next.

## Product layers

| Layer | Contents | State |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Knowledge | Investors, sources, passages, themes, concepts, companies, events, decisions, statistics, graph | Live |
| Research | Search, browse, compare, trails, decisions, timelines, cross-links | Live |
| Personal | Follow, continue, saved, new-since-visit, history, home | Live |
| Evidence | Source → Passage → Person → Original source chain; verification states; corrections; changelog | Live |
| AI | Interface over the evidence graph | Deferred |

The graph is the infrastructure. Search, compare, trails, decisions, and
eventually AI all consume the same graph; nothing duplicates it. The 31
investors are one interconnected corpus, entered through four entry points —
Investor, Theme/Concept, Company, Event — not a list of biographies.

## Free and Pro

Free = experience the graph. Pro = make it yours.

- Free readers get a bounded view of every public page: a teaser of passages
  ("Showing 5 of 37 references"), full reference counts, public trails, basic
  investor pages, and basic compare.
- Pro is a research system, not more paragraphs: the complete library, full
  decision ledgers, unlimited compare, follow with alerts, saved searches,
  collections, new-since-visit, research trails, and reading continuity.

Pricing is USD $19/month or $149/year, and INR ₹999/month or ₹7,999/year, by
region.

## Non-goals

Until their stated milestones are reached, Investor/Pass is not:

- A RAG, chatbot, or AI-opinion product. Blocked until 100 paying subscribers.
- An enterprise or team product.
- A verbatim archive of copyrighted documents. The paraphrase model is a legal
  and editorial stance, not a limitation to engineer around.
- An advice or prediction service. Everything is historical reference material
  for the study of investor thinking.
- A library-expansion project. 31 investors is enough; the objective is to
  make the existing library behave like one interconnected research machine.

## Competitive rule

> BuffettKnowledge-level knowledge infrastructure + cross-investor research +
> decisions + personal continuity.

Per-investor depth is table stakes. The differentiators are cross-investor
navigation, the documented decision record, and the personal layer that
remembers a researcher's place across sessions.

## The AI rule

> AI is an interface over the evidence graph, not the evidence graph itself —
> and it does not ship publicly until Investor/Pass reaches 100 subscribers.

Until then, no AI surfaces in the product, and no AI-generated claims in the
corpus. Agents may propose; humans verify.
